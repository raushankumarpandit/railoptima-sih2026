## 3. Recommended 11-Stage Demonstration Sequence

This sequential walkthrough demonstrates the end-to-end decision-support flow:
$$\text{Data Ingestion} \longrightarrow \text{Asset Telemetry} \longrightarrow \text{ML Inference} \longrightarrow \text{Optimization} \longrightarrow \text{Planner Action} \longrightarrow \text{Audit Recording}$$

---

### Step 1: Secure Planner Authentication
- **Action:** Open `http://localhost:5173` in a web browser. Enter username `planner` and password `planner123`. Click **Sign In**.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - JWT token issued via FastAPI backend (`/auth/login`).
  - Access granted to the section controller operations portal with role privileges.
  - Active environment banner indicating prototype synthetic data mode for the Delhi–Ghaziabad corridor.

---

### Step 2: Network Overview & Corridor Telemetry
- **Action:** Navigate to the **Network Overview** tab (default dashboard).
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Interactive schematic topology of the $28.5\text{ km}$ Delhi–Ghaziabad corridor across 6 stations: Old Delhi Junction (DLI), Delhi Shahdara (DSA), Chander Nagar (CNJ), Anand Vihar (ANVR), Sahibabad (SBB), and Ghaziabad Junction (GZB).
  - High-level KPIs: Total canonical assets (750), critical asset count, attention backlog count, and average corridor asset health index ($0–100$).
  - Section-level breakdown showing operational track codes (`SEC-001` through `SEC-006`).

---

### Step 3: Asset Intelligence & Health Diagnostics
- **Action:** Click on **Asset Intelligence** in the sidebar navigation.
  - Type `TRK` or `SIG` into the search bar (or press `/` shortcut).
  - Filter by risk level: **Critical**, **Attention**, or **Healthy**.
  - Click on any asset row (e.g., an asset with high urgency) to open the Asset Detail Modal.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Multi-sensor degradation telemetry: vibration ($\text{mm/s}$), rail wear ($\text{mm}$), ultrasonic flaw score, and track geometry deviation ($\text{mm}$).
  - Maintenance history showing previous servicing outcomes and days elapsed since last scheduled overhaul.
  - Live machine learning inference: Failure probability and Urgency score generated by the trained `GradientBoostingClassifier`.
  - Direct "Plan Block for Asset" trigger button leading into the Block Planner.

---

### Step 4: Section Traffic Forecast & Operational Load
- **Action:** Navigate to the **Traffic Forecast** tab.
  - Select Section `SEC-001` (DLI–DSA UP) and Date `2026-08-01`.
  - Review the 24-hour hourly train count timeline.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Hour-by-hour operational density derived from the canonical goods-train forecast.
  - Categorization into discrete operational tiers:
    - **LOW ($\le 3\text{ trains/hr}$):** Prime possession candidate windows (typically late night / early morning).
    - **MEDIUM ($4–9\text{ trains/hr}$):** Constrained windows requiring speed moderation.
    - **HIGH ($> 9\text{ trains/hr}$):** Peak operational intervals where possessions cause severe delays.
  - Clear labeling that train counts reflect operational freight forecast data while the classifier evaluates density levels.

---

### Step 5: Maintenance Backlog Prioritization
- **Action:** Select **Maintenance Backlog** from the sidebar.
  - Filter tasks by status and urgency category.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Consolidated list of pending inspection, preventive, and corrective work orders.
  - Backlog ranking combining asset physical degradation score with days elapsed since mandatory servicing.
  - Clear visibility into deferred maintenance risks, highlighting sections in urgent need of track access.

---

### Step 6: Optimal Block Planner Configuration
- **Action:** Open the **Optimal Block Planner** page.
  - Target Section: Select `SEC-001` (or click directly from an asset).
  - Maintenance Type: Select `PREVENTIVE` or `TRACK_RENEWAL`.
  - Date: Select `2026-08-01`.
  - Block Duration: Select `3 hours`.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Interactive parameter inputs matching actual railway possession request requisitions.
  - Real-time pre-validation of constraints (duration between 1 and 12 hours).

---

### Step 7: Generate Optimal Block Recommendation
- **Action:** Click the **Generate Optimal Block** button.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Multi-phase evaluation sequence:
    1. Evaluating asset condition & failure risks.
    2. Checking corridor traffic operating pressure.
    3. Ranking candidate possession windows.
    4. Calculating corridor disruption minimization.
  - Deterministic evaluation of candidate slots (e.g., `00:00–03:00`, `02:00–05:00`, `05:00–08:00`, `10:00–13:00`, `13:00–16:00`, `21:00–00:00`).
  - Identification of the optimal window maximizing the composite priority objective:
    $$\text{Priority} = 1.6 \times \text{Urgency} + 0.15 \times \min(\text{Backlog}, 60) - 1.0 \times \text{Disruption}$$

---

### Step 8: Explainable Recommendation & Candidate Trade-offs
- **Action:** Inspect the **Recommendation Details & Explainability Card**.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - **Optimal Slot Identified:** Displays the recommended window label (e.g., `02:00–05:00`), predicted traffic, and estimated disruption in minutes.
  - **Natural Language Rationale:** Clear textual explanation stating asset urgency, failure risk, and traffic density.
  - **Quantified Advantage:** Delay reduction percentage compared to the runner-up alternative slot.
  - **Ranked Alternatives Table:** Transparent side-by-side comparison of all evaluated candidate windows showing traffic levels and composite priority scores.
  - **Feature Importance Indicators:** Visual attribution of what factors drove the recommendation (historical failures, duration, emergency history, days since service).

---

### Step 9: Human-in-the-Loop Planner Decision
- **Action:** Review the action buttons on the recommendation card:
  - Click **Approve Block** (or **Reject Block** if simulating a conflict).
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - The system adheres strictly to *"AI recommends. The planner decides."*
  - Instant status transition to `APPROVED` or `REJECTED`.
  - Backend records timestamp (`decided_at`) and planner identity (`decided_by`).
  - Confirmation toast notification confirming that the block decision has been committed to the official database.

---

### Step 10: Block Decisions Ledger & Execution Audit
- **Action:** Navigate to the **Block Decisions** (or **Recommendations History**) page.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Historical chronological ledger of all generated block requests and their decision states (`PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `COMPLETED`).
  - Full auditability: Track section, requested date, duration, recommended slot, decision-maker username, and approval timestamp.
  - Feedback submission capability: Field engineers can record actual start/end times and actual delay minutes to track variance against predictions.

---

### Step 11: System & Model Analytics
- **Action:** Navigate to the **System Analytics** page.
- **Reviewer Observation:**
  - Live model metrics loaded directly from saved model artifacts (`urgency_metrics.json` and `traffic_metrics.json`):
    - Urgency Classifier: 98.0% Accuracy, 93.1% Precision, 96.4% Recall, 94.7% F1, 99.27% ROC-AUC.
    - Health Regression: $R^2 = 0.9813$, $\text{MAE} = 1.0375$.
    - Traffic Density Model: 96.49% Accuracy.
  - Operational KPIs: Total recommendations generated, approval rate percentage, and average estimated delay reduction achieved across the corridor.
